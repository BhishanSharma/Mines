# Market, Popularity and Monetization Potential for "Mines" (Minesweeper-style Android game)

Context: indie Android game "Mines" (Kotlin/Compose, 3-person team, not yet launched). It already has coins, a hearts/lives energy system, a cosmetic store, achievements and a tournaments screen. No ads or billing are integrated yet. Research date: 2026-11-06. Older data is labeled with its year. Casino "Mines" games are excluded except for the name-collision note.

Source-quality warning: most benchmark numbers below come from vendor blogs or secondary summaries (Playio, Segwise, Mobidictum), not from primary report PDFs. Treat them as directional. Figures that conflict are flagged.

## Mobile puzzle game market size and trends 2024-2026; casual/logic puzzle share

### Takeaway
Puzzle is the largest and only growing top casual genre, at about $8.2B IAP in 2025 (+7.6% YoY). That money is concentrated in match-3 and merge titles run by large studios. Logic and Minesweeper-style games are a small niche with no published revenue breakdown, and they earn mostly from ads rather than IAP.

### Cited Findings
- AppMagic 2025 casual report: Puzzle earned $8.2B revenue (+7.6% YoY) with downloads +9.9%, the only top casual genre that grew. Casino earned $6.9B (-7.6%; the same article's takeaways say $6.2B, an internal conflict) and Simulation $1.9B. Puzzle and Casino together hold more than 72% of casual revenue. — [Mobidictum / AppMagic casual report 2025](https://mobidictum.com/?p=210271)
- Puzzle subgenres in 2025: Match-3 $4.8B (flat, about 59% of puzzle), Merge $1.4B (+80%), Match-2 Blast $466M, Sort $279M, Screw $177M, Block puzzles $213M (about 10x YoY). Logic, brain, sudoku and Minesweeper are not broken out. — [Mobidictum / AppMagic](https://mobidictum.com/?p=210271)
- Concentration: only 4 of 367 new match-3 launches passed $100K monthly revenue even once. Dream Games holds more than 30% of match-3 revenue. — [Mobidictum / AppMagic](https://mobidictum.com/?p=210271)
- Royal Match was the top-grossing puzzle title of 2025, with more than $1.4B IAP (Sensor Tower). — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/what-leading-match-3-and-merge-games-do-differently/)
- All puzzle genres combined reached $9.86B for June 2025 to May 2026, +14.7% YoY (attributed to AppMagic, but via a LinkedIn post, so weak sourcing). — [LinkedIn heatmap post](https://vn.linkedin.com/in/halotran)
- Sensor Tower Q2 2025: puzzle IAP revenue was up about 13% YoY, second only to strategy (secondary summary). — [games.gg](https://games.gg/hi/news/top-mobile-games-in-q2-2025)
- In H1 2025, casual game downloads rose 6% YoY while revenue largely plateaued. — [Mobile Marketing Reads](https://mobilemarketingreads.com/?p=48297)
- Mobile accounted for 55% of total games revenue in 2025. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mobile-accounts-for-55-of-total-games-revenue-in-2025-as-studios-shift-towards-retention)
- "Hybridcasual" puzzles (simple core loop, IAP plus ads) are described as the main competitive area heading into 2026. — [Mobidictum / AppMagic](https://mobidictum.com/?p=210271)
- A 2024 figure (Foxdata) puts puzzle mobile revenue at $6.05B, +48.88% YoY. Its scope is unclear and it does not match AppMagic's scope. — [Foxdata](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/puzzle-mobile-games-in-2025-market-overview-user-acquisition-strategies/)
- Research-aggregator market sizes (for example $18.6B "puzzle games market" in 2025, all platforms) vary widely and are low quality. — [Marketintelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/puzzle-games-market)

### Inferences
- The headline $8-10B puzzle market is mostly irrelevant to Mines. Mines' realistic segment is the ad-monetized "classic logic" long tail (Minesweeper, Sudoku, Nonogram), where per-title revenue is small.
- The growth in hybridcasual puzzles (sort, screw, block) shows that a simple core loop combined with progression and meta (which Mines already has: coins, cosmetics, achievements) can work. Those successes, however, were driven by paid user acquisition at scale.

### Gaps
- No public revenue or download figure for the "logic puzzle" or Minesweeper subgenre specifically (AppMagic and Sensor Tower data are behind paywalls).
- No primary Sensor Tower or data.ai 2026 full-year puzzle figure was found.

## Benchmarks: retention, eCPMs, ARPDAU, IAP conversion, CPI, indie install distribution

### Takeaway
Typical mobile games retain about 22% at D1, 4% at D7 and 0.7% at D30, while puzzle games sit at the stronger end. Android rewarded ads pay about $9-13 eCPM in North America but $2-5 elsewhere, and banners pay under $1. A realistic hybrid ARPDAU for a small logic game is $0.02-0.05. Most indie titles with no marketing stay under 10K installs.

### Cited Findings
Retention
- GameAnalytics 2025 benchmarks (2024 data; 11,600 apps, 1.48B MAU): D1 top-25% is 26.5-27.7% (bottom 25% is 10-11.5%), Android top-25% D1 25-27%; median D7 3.4-3.9%, top-25% D7 7-8%; 75% of projects are below 3% at D28. Puzzle is "among the best genres for medium- and long-term retention." Median session is 5-6 min, about 4 sessions per day. — [GameDev Reports / GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks)
- Another summary of GameAnalytics data: median D1 about 22%, D7 about 4%, D30 about 0.7%; top 10% D1 40%, D7 12%, D30 4%. — [search summary of GameAnalytics coverage](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)
- GameAnalytics Q1 2024: puzzle had the highest D7 retention of all genres. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-benchmarks-in-mobile)
- A Mistplay-sourced puzzle genre table: D1 31.85%, D7 12.18%, D30 5.35% (third party, unverified, likely skewed to Mistplay users). — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/mobile-gaming-app-user-retention-strategies.md)

Ad eCPMs (Android unless noted)
- Appodeal Q4 2024, Android rewarded: North America about $9.0, Europe $5.1, APAC $8.2, Middle East $2.4, LatAm $1.8. Android interstitial: North America $9.6, Europe $3.6, APAC $5.5, Middle East $1.6, LatAm $1.3. US Android rewarded about $13.2, US interstitial about $12.3. North America Android banner about $0.5. — [Playio summarizing Appodeal](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026)
- Tenjin x CAS Q2 2024, Android games: rewarded US $30.25 / Canada $23.13 / South Korea $22.01; interstitial US $16.15; banner Canada $2.00 / US $1.18. These are much higher than Appodeal's figures. — [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026)
- Playwire 2025 (methodology undisclosed): rewarded Tier-1 $15-30, global $8-18; interstitial Tier-1 $5-8; banner $0.2-1.5. — [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026)
- TopOn H1 2025: Android rewarded and interstitial eCPM down 7% and 11% YoY, near three-year lows. Format ranking: rewarded > interstitial > splash > native > banner. — [TopOn report PDF](https://mores.toponad.com/reports/TopOn%20Global%20Mobile%20Games%20Monetization%20Report%20_%202025%20H1.pdf)

ARPDAU, IAP, CPI
- ARPDAU guide (2026): hybrid-casual (ads + IAP) $0.15-0.50 with a 40-60% IAP share; hypercasual $0.03-0.08. — [Playio ARPDAU benchmarks](https://blog.playio.co/arpdau-benchmarks-mobile-games)

- Top-tier puzzle IAP ARPDAU in 2025: Royal Match $0.17, Candy Crush $0.11 (Sensor Tower-based). — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/what-leading-match-3-and-merge-games-do-differently/)
- CPI: US casual/puzzle Android about $2.00 vs iOS $3.00 (2024) — [Mapendo](https://mapendo.co/blog/mobile-games-cpi-2025). Global Android CPI $1.22 (late 2024 to early 2025) — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/cpi-ipm-roas-benchmarks-optimizing-ad-spend/). US casual Android $1.50-3.50 (2026) — [Playio CPI benchmarks](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-cpi-benchmarks-2026). Statista reports $0.14 average Android casual CPI (Feb 2024 to Feb 2025), which is inconsistent with the other sources. — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1241651/global-cpi-casual-gaming-apps-genre-platform)
- No credible public puzzle-specific IAP payer-conversion rate was found.

Indie install distribution and postmortems
- AppBrain: "the vast majority of all downloads is generated by a relatively small number of popular apps." No public under-1K share was found. — [AppBrain stats](https://www.appbrain.com/stats/libraries/ad)
- GravityShot (small Godot puzzle game, almost no marketing): about 50 downloads and $0.10 revenue. — [postmortem](https://gamedo-live.pages.dev/news/gravityshot-postmortem-shipping-mobile-game-godot)
- Starlight X-2 (mobile puzzle): 9,970 Google Play downloads in 6 months (older, about 2013-2015). — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/revenue-expenses-and-statistics-of-starlight-x-2-a-mobile-puzzle-game)
- Mahjong Cards (2015): about €7,400 first-year ad revenue, mostly AdMob interstitials. — [hollance blog](https://hollance.github.io/2015/05/27/mahjong-cards-the-first-year)
- 1010! (Gram Games; Google-published case study, older): more than 5M Google Play downloads; $5K earnings in a single day after moving to AdMob. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/admob/gram-games-growing-revenue-7x-wi)

### Inferences
- For Mines, realistic targets are D1 25-35%, D7 8-12% and D30 3-5%, if onboarding and the hearts system do not choke early sessions. Benchmarks suggest puzzle is above the overall median.
- Hybrid-casual ARPDAU of $0.15-0.50 applies to games with deep IAP economies. A Minesweeper game with light IAP should plan on $0.02-0.06, mostly from rewarded ads plus one interstitial at natural breaks. Its traffic will be global with a heavy Tier-3 share, which lowers blended eCPM to roughly $2-5 for rewarded.
- At $1.50-3.50 US Android CPI, paid user acquisition is not ROI-positive at $0.03 ARPDAU and about 5-8 lifetime days per install (LTV of roughly $0.15-0.40). Growth must be organic: ASO, Reddit and community, featuring, and tournaments.

### Gaps
- Puzzle-specific D1/D7/D30 from a primary GameAnalytics report was not found.
- No puzzle IAP conversion rate. General industry folklore (1-3% payers) could not be sourced, so it is not used as fact.
- No market-wide indie install distribution (share of apps under 1K or 10K installs).
- Primary Liftoff, AppLovin and Unity 2025/2026 reports were not fetched.

## Player sentiment on energy/hearts systems in logic puzzle games

### Takeaway
The evidence is anecdotal but consistent: players of puzzle games often call energy limits and slow regeneration the top complaint in reviews, and say they feel punitive in skill or logic games. Designers who keep energy systems delay the constraint, reward success, and offer refills through ads or social features. No controlled study quantifies the retention impact.

### Cited Findings
- In a mystery puzzle app, most reviews were about how poor the energy system was. Developers promised fixes, but nothing changed. — [Marlvel review analysis (Unsolved)](https://marlvel.ai/apps/com-artifexmundi-unsolved/reviews)
- A picture puzzle app's review summary: energy limits and slow regeneration prevent long sessions, and the bar drains too fast. — [Marlvel (Picture Builder)](https://marlvel.ai/apps/picture-builder-puzzle-game/reviews)
- An itch.io player on a puzzle game: the energy mechanic was "more annoying" than something to plan around, since running out came from a single mistake. — [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/3708783)
- Design guidance: the best energy systems reward success rather than punishing failure. — [Adrian Crook](https://adriancrook.com/?p=8174)
- Common pattern: players know about the energy system but don't hit its limit in their first session. Royal Match's lives screen shows the time to the next life, lets players request lives from their team, and offers a paid refill. — [Mechanics Playbook](https://mechanics-playbook-next.pages.dev/mechanics/energy-lives/)
- Counterpoint: energy systems teach session length and encourage daily return (Hogwarts Mystery column). — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/harry-potter-hogwarts-mystery/making-the-case-for-harry-potter-hogwarts-mysterys-energy-system/)

### Inferences
- Minesweeper is especially at risk. Losses come from 50/50 guesses (luck), so losing a heart on a forced guess feels unfair. Classic Minesweeper players expect unlimited play, and every top competitor offers it.
- Recommendation: keep classic modes and difficulties unlimited (no hearts). Apply hearts only to a progression or "campaign" or tournament mode. Alternatively, use hearts as a "continue after hitting a mine" resource rather than an entry fee. Allow refills by rewarded ad and regenerate generously. Add a no-guess board generator to reduce perceived unfairness.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on how energy systems affect ratings or retention in logic games specifically.

## Monetization model fit and mapping of Mines' existing systems

### Takeaway
The best fit is ad-first hybrid monetization: rewarded ads as the core, a few interstitials, a "Remove Ads" IAP, plus cosmetics and coin packs. Premium and subscription models do not fit a commodity genre where competitors are free. Mines' coins, hearts, store and tournaments map cleanly onto rewarded-ad placements and small IAPs.

### Cited Findings
- Category norm: Minesweeper clones on Play are mostly ad-funded (for example ABCTech: 100% ads, 0% IAP). — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.raymond.minesweeper)
- Rewarded ads have the highest eCPM, banners the lowest in every region. — [TopOn H1 2025](https://mores.toponad.com/reports/TopOn%20Global%20Mobile%20Games%20Monetization%20Report%20_%202025%20H1.pdf); [Appodeal Q4 2024](https://appodeal.com/the-mobile-ecpm-report-updated-q4-2024-view)
- Paid "Pro" versions exist (Minesweeper Pro, more than 1M downloads), but their reach was largely driven by "temporarily free" promotions, and one is listed at ₹500. — [giga.de](https://www.giga.de/news/statt-1-59-euro-aktuell-kostenlos-android-app-bringt-legendaereres-pc-spiel-auf-euer-handy/); [Google Play search](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=minesweeper&c=apps&hl=en_US)
- Hybridcasual (IAP plus ads) puzzles are the growth segment for 2025-2026. — [Mobidictum / AppMagic](https://mobidictum.com/?p=210271)
- AdMob interstitials made up most of the first-year revenue for an indie tile puzzle (2015). — [hollance](https://hollance.github.io/2015/05/27/mahjong-cards-the-first-year)

### Inferences (mapping)
| Mines system | Monetization hook | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hearts/lives | Rewarded ad = +1 heart or a "second chance" after hitting a mine; IAP heart refill or unlimited hearts for 1h | Only in progression or tournament modes; keep classic free |
| Coins | Earned by play, achievements and daily login; rewarded ad doubles end-of-level coins; IAP coin packs ($0.99-$4.99) | Spend on cosmetics, hints and tournament entries |
| Cosmetic store (themes, mine/flag skins) | Coin purchases plus premium-only cosmetics; IAP bundles | Safest IAP, no pay-to-win concerns in a logic game |
| Hints / "safe reveal" | Rewarded ad or coins | Natural fit for 50/50 situations |
| Achievements | Grant coins and cosmetics (retention, not direct revenue) | Use Play Games Services |
| Tournaments | Daily/weekly seeded boards with leaderboards; coin-entry or rewarded-ad entry; cosmetic prizes | Strong differentiation for retention and UGC/social sharing |
| Interstitial | After every 2-3 completed games, never mid-game; frequency cap | Removed by "Remove Ads" IAP |
| Remove Ads IAP ($2.99-$4.99) | Removes interstitials and banners but keeps optional rewarded ads | Highest-intent purchase for classic-puzzle audiences |
| Optional VIP or season pass | Later, only if D30 > 5% | A subscription is unlikely to fit this scale |

- Banners: avoid them, or use them only on menus. Their value is low (about $0.5 North America) and they hurt the "clean" positioning that top-rated competitors use.
- Integration needed: AdMob (or AppLovin MAX / LevelPlay mediation), Google Play Billing Library, UMP consent (GDPR), and a Firebase/GameAnalytics SDK to measure retention and ARPDAU.

### Gaps
- No public data on remove-ads conversion in puzzle games.

## 12-month forecast (pessimistic / base / optimistic)

### Takeaway
Without a paid user acquisition budget, the most likely 12-month result is about 10K-30K installs, a few hundred DAU, and low thousands of USD in revenue. Six-figure revenue requires a breakout, such as featuring, virality or a successful tournament loop, and is not a plan.

### Cited Findings (inputs used)
- Retention ranges: GameAnalytics medians and top quartiles (see above). — [GameDev Reports / GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks)
- eCPM ranges: Appodeal Q4 2024 regional Android figures. — [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026)
- Indie outcomes: about 50 installs (GravityShot) to about 10K in 6 months (Starlight X-2). Competitor clones sit at 10K-500K installs after years. — [GravityShot](https://gamedo-live.pages.dev/news/gravityshot-postmortem-shipping-mobile-game-godot); [Starlight X-2](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/revenue-expenses-and-statistics-of-starlight-x-2-a-mobile-puzzle-game); [Google Play listings above](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hyperlite.minesweeper&hl=en)

### Inferences (model; all assumptions are mine, not sourced)
Method: average DAU ≈ installs × lifetime active days ÷ 365. Revenue = DAU-days × ARPDAU + IAP. Google takes a 15% fee on the first $1M of IAP. Android only, no paid user acquisition.

| Scenario | 12-mo installs | Retention D1/D7/D30 | Lifetime active days per install | Avg DAU (peak) | Blended ARPDAU | Ad revenue | IAP (remove-ads/cosmetics) | Total 12-mo gross |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic | 2,000 | 15% / 3% / 0.7% | 2 | ~10 (~50) | $0.01 | ~$40 | ~10 buyers × $3 ≈ $25 | **< $100** |
| Base | 20,000 | 28% / 8% / 3% | 6 | ~330 (~800) | $0.03 | ~$3,600 | 0.5% of installs × $3.50 ≈ $300 net | **~$3-5K** |
| Optimistic | 250,000 (featuring or viral tournament/Reddit moment) | 35% / 12% / 5% | 10 | ~6,800 (~15K) | $0.05 (more Tier-1, more rewarded views) | ~$125K | 1% × $4 ≈ $8.5K net | **~$100-140K** |

Key assumptions:
- Ad ARPDAU: about 2-4 rewarded views + 1-3 interstitials per DAU, blended global rewarded eCPM $3-6 (mixed Tier-1 and Tier-3 traffic).
- Most installs come from Play search for "minesweeper." ASO title must include "Minesweeper."
- The hearts system does not gate the classic modes. If it does, assume the pessimistic retention.
- No iOS version. Adding iOS would roughly add 30-60% of revenue at a higher eCPM.
- Break-even view: if 3 developers value their time at even $1K/month each, only the optimistic case covers costs. Treat Mines as a portfolio or learning title unless early D1/D7 beat the top quartile in a soft launch.

### Gaps
- Install volume from organic Play search for "minesweeper" (keyword volume) is unknown. AppTweak or Sensor Tower keyword data would firm up the base case.
- Forecast figures are illustrative arithmetic, not sourced predictions.

## Note: casino "Mines" name collision
- "Mines" is a well-known real-money casino game (Spribe; Stake Originals), described as Minesweeper with gambling. — [Complete Sports](https://www.completesports.com/reviews/stake/mines/); [Gigwise](https://www.gigwise.com/?p=61934)
- Google Play restricts real-money gambling apps to approved developers in select countries. — [Google Play gambling policy](https://play.google.com/intl/en/about/restricted-content/gambling/)
- Inference: searches for "mines" will surface casino content and confuse the audience, and a coin-and-"mines" theme could look gambling-adjacent to reviewers or ad networks. Recommend a distinct title with "Minesweeper" in it, avoiding any bet/multiplier/cash-out language, and no real-money or random paid loot mechanics.
